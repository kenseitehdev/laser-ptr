# Changelog

## v0.2

- Add a native menu-bar status item with a high-contrast pointer glyph: white in dark mode and black in light mode.
- Light the pointer's red beam while keep-awake is active.
- Keep the system and display awake indefinitely or for 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours.
- End the keep-awake activity when its timer expires, when switched off, or when the app quits.
- Package as an Apple silicon accessory app for macOS 13 and newer.
